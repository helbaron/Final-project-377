About Future Saver

Future Saver is a personal finance dashboard designed for young adults who are beginning their financial journey. The platform helps users manage savings goals, debt, investments, and spending habits through one centralized and easy-to-use system.

The Mission

Future Saver was created to help young adults build better financial habits early in life. Many people in their 20s begin earning income for the first time but struggle with budgeting, saving money, managing debt, and understanding investments. Our goal is to provide a simple, centralized platform where users can track savings goals, monitor debt, manage investments, and gain a clearer understanding of their overall financial health in one place.

🎯

Savings Goals

Set meaningful savings goals with target dates and amounts. Track how much you've saved and how far you have left to go.

💳

Debt Tracker

Keep track of all your debts in one place. See your payoff progress and stay motivated as your balances go down.

↗️

Investments

Log your total invested amount, yearly return, and credit score. Get a full picture of your financial growth over time.

Live Stock Market

View real-time prices and 30-day trends for AAPL, MSFT, TSLA, and NVDA — powered by the Financial Modeling Prep API.

AI Insights

Get a personalized financial insight based on your savings rate and monthly contributions automatically on your dashboard.

🔒

Secure & Private

Your data is stored securely in Supabase. Only you can access your financial profile using your unique user session.
